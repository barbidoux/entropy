## The frame rate under the hose

With 1 M particles the hose's 10 m/s jet keeps the liquid at its 4-substep cap. The
simulation stage's GPU span is then 23.1 ms officially (23–24 ms unofficially), above SPEC 3's whole 22 ms simulation
budget; frames take about 25 ms (median 39–41 fps, p10 35–36 fps) and the world runs at
0.93–0.95× real time (unofficial runs of the final test). The rest of play (a dam break,
the bucket, the flood) runs at 60 fps.

A run at 13:37 read hose p10 29.8 fps. It was first put down to the GPU's heat, and
Matt accepted the gate on that account (« J'accepte le jalon », 2026-11-22). #80's
review then found Windows' screensaver drawing on the GPU during that run (started
13:34, while Matt was away). The run is set aside; with it gone the gate passes. Matt
is told the corrected facts. The hose's cost still has to come down before M3 shares
the GPU (#81), and the harness has to detect other work on the GPU (#82).

- **No NaN, leak or crash** in either run: every frame completed, and the particles
  were finite at the end. In the second run the NaN guard was checked after every frame
  and no dump appeared. For the first run, `out/nan_dumps` was looked at afterwards: its
  dumps (19 KB, from the test suite's NaN guard tests) date from before and after the
  soak's minutes, none from them.
- **Memory**: growth within 64 MiB in both runs. The second run's process figure,
  −30.8 MiB, came from the baseline: it was sampled on the frame of the first action
  after the settling minute (F9, in a 150 s trial of #80's review), and F9's restore holds about 32 MiB of host
  memory for about 3 s (#80's review logged it frame by frame, #84). It was a
  transient, not memory given back, and it let a leak of up to about 95 MiB pass. The
  soak now samples in quiet windows, at least 4 s after the last action, and also counts
  the checkpoint ring at both samples. An unofficial 125 s run of this version read
  +1.0 MiB (ring +0.0 MiB). An official run of this version is owed (#85).
- **V-LIQ-02 over 10 minutes**: the settled water read 0.9913–0.9973 of its particles'
  volume in the second run. The first window was 0.9928, within 1 % of 1; the others
  were within −0.15 % to +0.45 % of it.
- **Margins**: the settled volume sits about 0.6 % under the particles' volume, the
  film of particles above the surface and the volume correction's dead band (ADR 0018,
  ADR 0028), not water lost. Both criteria are anchored on the first window, so a first
  window near 0.99 leaves little room for a later one near 1.00: in the first run the
  first window was 0.9904, and an unofficial 240 s run of #80's review reached 0.9997
  after a first window of 0.9905, 0.07 point from the limit.
- **The water band**: the tools steer the water towards 480,000–600,000 particles; it
  ended at 447,438 and 406,693, below it after the last actions.
- **The GPU was otherwise idle** in both runs (12:27–12:37 and 13:20–13:30): the
  screensaver started at 13:34.
